The Issue

Food insecurity continues to affect children and families worldwide

Food insecurity is more than the feeling of hunger. It can affect a child’s health, education, focus, confidence, and future opportunities. When children do not have consistent access to nutritious food, it can impact the way they learn, grow, and participate in daily life.

Around the world, many families continue to face challenges caused by poverty, rising food costs, climate issues, and limited access to resources. These problems make it harder for children and communities to receive the basic nutrition and support they need.

About Project Navya

Learning before leading

Project Navya is a youth ambassador program created to help students learn about the Children of the Mother Soil initiative, raise awareness, and lead respectful fundraising campaigns.

Through this program, interns build communication, leadership, and outreach skills while supporting a cause focused on helping children in India.

All proceeds raised through Project Navya will go toward the Children of the Mother Soil initiative through the approved donation method.

About Project Navya

Why this program exists

Project Navya was created to give students a meaningful way to learn about food insecurity, understand its impact, and turn awareness into action. The program is designed for students who want to build leadership skills while supporting a real cause.

The goal of Project Navya is not just to ask people to donate. It is to help students understand the issue, communicate it respectfully, create thoughtful campaigns, and track the impact of their work.

Project Navya supports the Children of the Mother Soil initiative, also referred to as COMS in this program. COMS is the cause that Project Navya supports through student-led awareness and fundraising. Project Navya is not replacing COMS; it is a youth ambassador program created to raise support for that initiative.

All proceeds raised through Project Navya will go toward the Children of the Mother Soil initiative through the approved donation method.

Founder

Meet the Founder

My name is Nishtha Desai, and I am the founder of Project Navya - Youth Ambassador Program. I created this program because food insecurity is a cause I have spent time researching, learning about, and personally reflecting on.

In 10th grade, I was assigned food insecurity as my World Issues project. What started as a classroom topic became a year-long experience where I learned about hunger, its causes, its effects, and the way it continues to impact millions of people around the world.

Through my research, I learned that food insecurity is not just about missing one meal. It affects health, education, childhood development, family stability, and entire communities.

The more I learned, the more I realized that awareness matters. That experience inspired me to create Project Navya as a way for students to learn, lead, and turn awareness into action.

I created Project Navya because I wanted my research to become more than a paper. I wanted it to become action.

The Internship

What interns will do

Learn

Interns begin by learning about the cause, the children and communities connected to it, and the importance of respectful communication.

Create

Each intern designs an original awareness or fundraising campaign using posters, videos, presentations, social media, or community outreach.

Lead

Interns track their campaign progress, reflect on their work, and submit updates about what they learned and accomplished.

FAQ

Common questions

Does this program give community-service hours?

Yes. This program provides community-service hours for approved participation, campaign work, and completed program requirements.

Do interns need experience?

No. Interns only need a willingness to learn, communicate respectfully, and complete their campaign work honestly.

How will interns organize their work?

Program materials, announcements, assignments, and updates can be organized through Google Classroom.

Can interns work in teams?

Interns may work individually or in small teams; however, this must be coordinated with the program manager.

What kind of work will interns submit?

Interns may submit campaign plans, posters, videos, outreach updates, fundraising reflections, and a final summary of what they created and learned.

How are interns recognized?

Interns may be recognized through campaign completion, leadership effort, fundraising impact, reflection quality, and placement on the ambassador leaderboard.
